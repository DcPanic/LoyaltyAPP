☕ LoyaltyApp preview
This is a static preview with sample data — it runs entirely in your browser so you can click through the screens. The real platform runs on your own server with a database, genuine Apple and Google Wallet passes and live NFC tags. Everything you stamp here updates across all three views, exactly as it does in the real app.

The customer's phone — no app installed

How a customer joins and collects

They scan the QR on the counter, fill in their name, and the card lands in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. After that a tap on the NFC stamp is the whole interaction.

Try the NFC stamp

This is the 3D-printed stamp on the counter with a cheap NFC sticker inside. Tap it the way a customer would tap their phone on it.

Get the card first, then tap

What the wallet card shows

One card per café, updated on every stamp — never a new pass.

The barista's phone — React Native app or mobile web

Stamping at the counter, on the phone, for delivery

The barista scans the customer's QR, or searches by name or phone when the customer is not there — a telephone or delivery order is stamped the same way.

Staff see only what they need

Find a customer and add stampsallowed
Redeem a reward (if the owner enables it)allowed
Customer CRM, exports, analyticsblocked
Billing, settings, staff managementblocked

The server enforces this, not the screen: a blocked request is refused even if someone calls the API directly.

Every stamp is recorded once

A double tap, a retry on a flaky connection or a refreshed page cannot add a second stamp — each request carries an id the database enforces as unique.
